---
name: Generator file curriculum
description: The uploaded generator files are the source of truth for grade and subject assignments.
---

The SOMA curriculum catalog must follow the `grade` and `subject` fields in the uploaded generator files, not a separately supplied subject list. Exact names matter, and missing generator coverage for a grade is meaningful.

**Why:** The uploaded files contain grade-specific coverage that differs by subject; collapsing them into broad curriculum-level lists introduces subjects at grades where their generators do not exist.

**How to apply:** Extract the grade/subject pairs from the original generator files when changing the catalog. Preserve names such as `Christian Religious Education`, `Hygiene and Nutrition`, `Health Education`, `Creative Arts and Sports`, and `History and Citizenship`.
